Canary in a coal mine

Science

Category: Science科 学

In short

Something that warns of approaching danger earlier than anything else.

The story

Coal miners once carried caged canaries underground because the birds are sensitive to toxic gases like carbon monoxide. If a canary showed distress, the miners got out. The British physiologist John Scott Haldane is known for recommending the practice, and British mines didn’t replace canaries with electronic detectors until the 1980s.

The lens

The first person at work to fall ill, or the first to quit, may be the organization’s canary. Listening to the people who notice small changes helps protect everyone.

Drop it casually

“My sensitive junior colleague has seemed down lately. She’s our team’s canary in the coal mine, so let’s check in.”
