Word of the day for July 31, 2027 (Day 303)

Lipogram

Language

Category: Language言 葉

In short

A text written without ever using a particular letter.

The story

In 1969 the French writer Georges Perec wrote a whole novel, La Disparition, without once using “e,” the most common letter in French. It was translated into English, as A Void, also without an “e,” and the Japanese translation avoids an entire row of the kana syllabary, every syllable ending in an “i” sound.

The lens

Inside the novel, the missing “e” itself becomes a story of absence and loss that haunts the characters. It’s often read alongside Perec’s own life: he lost both his parents to the war as a small child. Constraints can make a text speak about what goes unsaid.

Drop it casually

“I tried to go a whole day without the letter e and couldn’t even say yes. A lipogram is a spiritual discipline.”
