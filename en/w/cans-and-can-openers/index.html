Cans and can openers

Food

Category: Food食

In short

A quirk of food history: nearly half a century passed between the invention of canned food and the arrival of the can opener.

The story

Around 1810, the Frenchman Nicolas Appert won a prize offered by Napoleon’s government for a way to preserve food, with a method of sealing food in jars and heating it. Around the same time a patent was granted in Britain for using tin cans, and canned food spread as rations for armies and expeditions. But cans back then were thick, and people pried them open with knives, or with a hammer and chisel.

The lens

Dedicated can openers appeared only in the 1850s, once cans could be made thinner and a tool to open them was worth designing. An invention doesn’t always come complete on its own. It takes root in daily life only when the means of using it catch up.

Drop it casually

“We installed a new system and nobody explained how to use it. It’s cans before can openers all over again.”
