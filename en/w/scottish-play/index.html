Word of the day for May 19, 2028 (Day 596)

The Scottish play

Literature

Category: Literature文 学

In short

The name actors use for Macbeth, born of the superstition that saying its title inside a theater brings disaster.

The story

Shakespeare’s Macbeth features witches’ incantations, and stories spread among actors that accidents and misfortunes followed its productions. So in theaters, people avoid the title and call it “the Scottish play.” Anyone who slips up is supposed to go outside, turn around three times, spit, or perform some other ritual.

The lens

Whatever the truth of the curse, avoiding the word probably helps share anxiety and confirm a sense of belonging. It’s not unlike the Japanese custom of never saying “fall” or “slip” around students about to take entrance exams.

Drop it casually

“Not saying “fail” the night before an exam is the test-taker’s version of the Scottish play.”
