Word of the day for June 25, 2028 (Day 633)

Algorithm

Math & Computing

Category: Math & Computing数 理情 報

In short

A method of calculation with clearly defined steps for solving a problem. The word comes from the name of a ninth-century mathematician.

The story

In the ninth century, al-Khwarizmi, a mathematician at the House of Wisdom in Baghdad, wrote a book explaining the numerals and methods of calculation that had come from India. When it was translated into Latin, it opened with “Thus spoke Algoritmi,” and his name eventually came to mean the method of calculation itself. “Algebra” comes from the title of another of his works.

The lens

Today even the systems that decide social media recommendations and the order of search results are called algorithms. Inside this word that shapes our days lives the name of a scholar from Baghdad more than a thousand years ago.

Drop it casually

“Every recommended video is about cats. Al-Khwarizmi, the man behind the word algorithm, never saw this coming.”
