Word of the day for December 13, 2027 (Day 438)

Thick description

Society

Category: Society社 会

In short

Describing human behavior not just by its surface movements but along with its context and meaning.

The story

The anthropologist Clifford Geertz popularized it in his 1973 book The Interpretation of Cultures. Borrowing an example from the philosopher Gilbert Ryle, he argued that the eye movements alone can’t tell you whether someone’s eyelid twitched, whether they winked as a signal, or whether they were parodying a wink, and that reading that meaning is the work of studying culture.

The lens

Being left on read may mean someone is busy, angry, or being considerate; it all depends on context. It’s a word that keeps us from thinking we understand people from data or behavior logs alone.

Drop it casually

“The survey numbers alone won’t tell us. We need thick description, so let’s go talk to people on the ground.”
