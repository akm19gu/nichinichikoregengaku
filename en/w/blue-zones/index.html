Word of the day for September 4, 2027 (Day 338)

Blue Zones

Medicine & Body

Category: Medicine & Body医 学か ら だ

In short

The name for regions such as Okinawa and Sardinia that are said to have unusually many people living past 100.

The story

The name is said to come from researchers in the early 2000s circling villages full of long-lived people in blue ink on a map. The American journalist Dan Buettner’s books, among others, made it widely known, presenting “secrets of longevity” in diet, exercise, and social ties.

The lens

The British researcher Saul Newman, on the other hand, argued that many records of extreme longevity may be errors stemming from poor birth records and pension fraud, and won an Ig Nobel Prize in 2024. When you meet an astonishing number, the first question is how solid the records are.

Drop it casually

“Villages of centenarians are romantic, but apparently the Blue Zones records have their doubters too. Question the numbers first.”
