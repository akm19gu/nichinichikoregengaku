Linear B

Language

Category: Language言 葉

In short

A script used in ancient Crete and mainland Greece, deciphered in the mid-20th century by an architect.

The story

In 1900 the British archaeologist Arthur Evans excavated large numbers of clay tablets inscribed in this script at the palace of Knossos on Crete. Evans believed it recorded a language other than Greek. In 1952 Michael Ventris, an architect who had been fascinated by the script since boyhood, deciphered it and showed it was the oldest known form of Greek.

The lens

What the deciphered tablets recorded wasn’t heroic tales but palace accounts: numbers of sheep and quantities of grain. People more than three thousand years ago were counting inventory every day too.

Drop it casually

“If someone reads our receipts three thousand years from now, will they look like Linear B ledgers?”
