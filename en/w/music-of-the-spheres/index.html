Music of the spheres

Music

Category: Music音 楽

In short

The ancient idea that the motions of the sun, moon, and planets sound beautiful harmonies too subtle for the ear to hear.

The story

The Pythagoreans of ancient Greece believed the order of the cosmos rested on the same numerical ratios as musical harmony. In the 17th century the astronomer Johannes Kepler wrote The Harmony of the World, likening the ratios of the planets’ speeds to musical intervals and even writing out each planet’s melody in notation. The same book contains what is now called Kepler’s third law.

The lens

A great scientific discovery was born from a dream of hearing the music of the universe. Under a starry sky, you may want to listen for those inaudible chords.

Drop it casually

“Watching the stars on a quiet night, I almost feel I could hear the music of the spheres.”
