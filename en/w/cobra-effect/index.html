Word of the day for January 6, 2027 (Day 97)

Cobra effect

Society

Category: Society社 会

In short

When a reward meant to solve a problem ends up making the problem worse.

The story

The name comes from a story set in British-ruled Delhi: to reduce venomous snakes, officials paid a bounty for dead cobras, people started breeding cobras for the money, and when the program was scrapped they released the snakes, leaving more than before. The tale itself is poorly documented, but in French-ruled Hanoi a bounty on rat tails is on record, and many people cut off the tails and let the rats go.

The lens

It’s the go-to example of a badly designed incentive. Pair it with Goodhart’s law and the idea that people act according to how they’re measured suddenly comes into focus.

Drop it casually

“If we pay a bonus for every bug found, won’t someone start planting bugs? I’m worried about a cobra effect.”
