Diabolus in musica

Art & Film

Category: Art & Film芸 術

In short

“The devil in music”: the nickname for the unsettling tritone, an interval three whole steps wide, such as F to B.

The story

It is often said that the medieval church banned the interval, but no clear ban has ever been found, and the nickname appears in writing mainly from the 18th century on. Still, its unstable sound made it something composers handled with care.

The lens

Its menace has been used for everything ominous, from Saint-Saëns’s Danse macabre to Black Sabbath’s riffs. Yet in “Maria” from West Side Story, the first two notes, “Ma-ri,” form a tritone that resolves on the very next note. Even the devil has his uses.

Drop it casually

“No wonder this background music feels uneasy. There’s a tritone in it. The devil in music.”
