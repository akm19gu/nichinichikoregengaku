Alan Smithee

Art & Film

Category: Art & Film芸 術

In short

A fictitious Hollywood director’s name used when a real director, unhappy with a film, didn’t want their name on it.

The story

It’s traced to the 1969 film Death of a Gunfighter, which changed directors during shooting. Neither director wanted to claim it, so the Directors Guild of America supplied an invented name. Many films were later credited to it.

The lens

Ironically, in 1997 a film was made about the system itself, and its director, unhappy with the edit, removed his name, so it really did become an Alan Smithee film. After that fiasco, the name is said to have been retired.

Drop it casually

“My boss rewrote this proposal so much it isn’t mine anymore. I want to sign it Alan Smithee.”
