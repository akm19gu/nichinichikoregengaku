Mpemba effect

Science

Category: Science科 学

In short

The claim that, under some conditions, warm water can freeze faster than cold water.

The story

In 1963, Erasto Mpemba, a secondary school student in Tanzania, put his ice cream mix in the freezer while it was still warm during a cooking class, and noticed it froze before his friends’ mix, which had been cooled first. Laughed at by his teacher, he kept at it and published a paper with the physicist Denis Osborne in 1969. Aristotle, Francis Bacon, and Descartes had all recorded similar observations.

The lens

The conditions for reproducing it are very delicate, and whether the effect really exists is still debated. You get to enjoy both the story of a mocked student whose observation became a paper and a question that remains open.

Drop it casually

“The ice tray filled with hot water freezes faster? The Mpemba effect isn’t settled yet, so it’s worth a try.”
