The van Meegeren affair

Han van Meegeren

Art & Film

Category: Art & Film芸 術

In short

The case of the Dutch painter Han van Meegeren, whose forged Vermeers fooled experts and even a top Nazi.

The story

After World War II, van Meegeren was arrested as a traitor for selling a Vermeer, a national treasure, to the Nazi leader Hermann Göring. He then confessed that the painting was his own forgery. When people didn’t believe him, he painted a new “Vermeer” under guard to prove it, escaping the charge of treason.

The lens

The man who chose to be tried as a forger rather than a traitor briefly became something of a national hero for having fooled the Nazis. A strange case in which genuine and fake, crime and achievement, swap places.

Drop it casually

“The fake ends up with a better reputation than the original? Sounds like the van Meegeren affair.”
