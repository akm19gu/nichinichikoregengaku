Dwarf male

Living Things

Category: Living Things生 き 物

In short

A male far smaller than the females of its species. In some deep-sea anglerfish, the male fuses with the female’s body.

The story

The deep sea is dark and vast, so males and females rarely meet. When a male of certain anglerfish species finds a female, he bites into her, and eventually their skin and even blood vessels join until he becomes part of her body. He gets nourishment from her and exists only to help produce offspring. A single female may carry several males.

The lens

Joining another individual’s body without triggering rejection is no small feat: in 2020 researchers reported that these fish have lost parts of their immune system. Nature has ways of “living together” beyond anything we’d imagine.

Drop it casually

“Those two are always together. Fused like an anglerfish’s dwarf male.”
