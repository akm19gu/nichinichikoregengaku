Genius loci

Architecture & Cities

Category: Architecture & Cities建 築都 市

In short

The distinctive atmosphere or character of a place. Originally, the ancient Roman name for the spirit that guarded a place.

The story

In Latin it means the protective spirit of a place. The Romans believed homes, groves, springs, and other places each had a guardian of their own and set up altars to them. In the 20th century, the Norwegian architectural theorist Christian Norberg-Schulz argued in a 1979 book that architecture should read the spirit of its site, making the phrase part of the architect’s vocabulary.

The lens

Why does the same chain store feel different from town to town? When a redevelopment leaves you feeling something has been lost, this gives that something a name.

Drop it casually

“The rebuilt station is more convenient, but its genius loci seems to have moved out.”
