Word of the day for November 2, 2026 (Day 32)

John Doe

Politics & Law

Category: Politics & Law政 治法

In short

A placeholder name used in American and British courts and police work for an unidentified person or a party who must stay anonymous.

The story

It is usually traced to land disputes in medieval England, where legal procedure required fictional plaintiffs and defendants, so the names John Doe and Richard Roe were used. For women, the name is Jane Doe.

The lens

Every language has its own. In Japan, the stand-in on every sample form is Tarō Yamada, and a nameless nobody is Nanashi no Gonbei, “Gonbei with no name.” You have probably heard John Doe used for an unidentified body in crime dramas.

Drop it casually

“Every sample form in Japan is filled out by a Tarō Yamada. He’s their hardest-working John Doe.”
