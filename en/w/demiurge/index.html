Demiurge

Religion & Myth

Category: Religion & Myth宗 教神 話

In short

A god as craftsman who shaped the world. In Gnostic belief, a lesser and flawed maker of an imperfect world, distinct from the true God.

The story

The Greek word means craftsman or artisan. In the Timaeus, Plato describes a good divine craftsman who shapes the cosmos out of existing material, using the eternal Forms as a model. Later Gnostic thinkers recast the demiurge as ignorant and arrogant, and blamed the flaws and evils of the material world on its maker’s defects.

The lens

It gives mythic form to the nagging feeling that the world is somehow badly made. The idea that the one who built the world is not the one truly worth worshipping still turns up in games and novels.

Drop it casually

“Nobody understands this internal system and whoever built it left years ago. We’re living in the demiurge’s world.”
