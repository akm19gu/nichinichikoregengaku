Point Nemo

Geography & Earth

Category: Geography & Earth地 理地 球

In short

The spot in the South Pacific farthest from any land. The nearest island is about 2,700 kilometers (1,670 miles) away.

The story

In 1992 the Canadian engineer Hrvoje Lukatela pinpointed it by computer. It’s named after Captain Nemo from Jules Verne’s Twenty Thousand Leagues Under the Seas, and nemo is also Latin for “no one.” With almost no people or ships nearby, it has served as a spacecraft graveyard where retired satellites and space stations are brought down. Russia’s Mir space station was sunk in the ocean near it in 2001.

The lens

The humans closest to it are often the astronauts aboard the International Space Station, orbiting about 400 kilometers overhead. The loneliest place on Earth sits just below space.

Drop it casually

“On days I don’t want to see anyone, I dream of Point Nemo. The place where your nearest neighbors are astronauts.”
