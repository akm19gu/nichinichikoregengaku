Ham sandwich theorem

Math & Computing

Category: Math & Computing数 理情 報

In short

However two slices of bread and a slice of ham are shaped and wherever they sit, a single straight cut can split all three exactly in half.

The story

The Polish mathematician Hugo Steinhaus posed the problem, and his colleague Stefan Banach is credited with proving it. It appears in the Scottish Book, the collection of problems they and their friends wrote down while gathering at a café in the city of Lwów. It was later generalized to any number of dimensions.

The lens

Even if the bread is in the corner of the room and the ham is on the table, one cut halves them all. The fun lies less in whether it’s useful than in being able to say it can always be done, which is very mathematical. The idea also feeds into research on fair division.

Drop it casually

“My kids always fight over how to split the sandwich. I want to teach them the ham sandwich theorem.”
