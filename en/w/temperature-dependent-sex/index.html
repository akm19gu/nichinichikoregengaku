Word of the day for April 17, 2028 (Day 564)

Temperature-dependent sex determination

Living Things

Category: Living Things生 き 物

In short

In sea turtles, crocodiles, and some other reptiles, the temperature of the sand or soil around the eggs determines whether the hatchlings are male or female.

The story

In many sea turtles, warmer nests produce more females and cooler nests more males, and the dividing line is just a degree or two. A 2018 study of green turtles hatched in the northern Great Barrier Reef of Australia found that nearly all the young turtles were female.

The lens

Rising global temperatures are starting to change the sex ratios of creatures like these. Climate change affects not just heat and rainfall but the very way life is born.

Drop it casually

“The temperature of the sand decides a sea turtle’s sex. Since learning about temperature-dependent sex determination, climate news looks different to me.”
