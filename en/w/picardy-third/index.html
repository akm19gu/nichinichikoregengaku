Word of the day for April 18, 2027 (Day 199)

Picardy third

Music

Category: Music音 楽

In short

Ending a piece in a minor key on a bright major chord.

The story

In European music from roughly the 16th to the 18th century, it was common to close a minor-key piece on a major chord. The name refers to the middle note of the final chord, the third, being raised a half step. Picardy is a region of northern France, but why the technique is named after it is unclear.

The lens

After a dark melody, the last chord feels like light breaking through. Bach used it often. It’s like the moment at the end of a sad film when a little grace appears.

Drop it casually

“The worst week ever, and then a friend called on Friday night and saved it. Life’s Picardy third.”
