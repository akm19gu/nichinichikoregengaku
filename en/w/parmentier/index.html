Word of the day for September 15, 2027 (Day 349)

Parmentier

Antoine-Augustin Parmentier

Food

Category: Food食

In short

The 18th-century French pharmacist who promoted the potato, then seen as animal feed, as food for people. His name lives on in potato dishes.

The story

Taken prisoner during a war, Parmentier survived on a diet of potatoes and took note of their nutrition. But in France at the time, potatoes were disliked and believed to cause disease. As the story goes, he had soldiers guard a potato field only during the day. People assumed anything so closely guarded must be valuable, and they crept in at night to steal the potatoes and plant them.

The lens

Don’t persuade; make people want it. How much of the story is true is unclear, but it’s still often cited as an example of how to move people. His name survives in hachis Parmentier, a baked dish of ground meat topped with mashed potato.

Drop it casually

“Tell me a document is off-limits and I want to read it. Parmentier’s potato strategy.”
