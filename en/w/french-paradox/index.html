Word of the day for June 16, 2028 (Day 624)

French paradox

Food

Category: Food食

In short

The apparent contradiction that the French eat a diet rich in butter, meat, and fat yet have relatively low rates of death from heart disease.

The story

In the early 1990s an American TV program covered the idea and suggested the answer might be red wine. Red wine sales in the US reportedly jumped after the broadcast.

The lens

Later research suggests much of it can be explained by differences in how statistics were gathered and by the time lag between changes in diet and rises in disease. Convenient health news spreads fastest. Before you raise your glass, a little doubt is in order.

Drop it casually

“Red wine is good for you? That comes from the French paradox. The more convenient the theory, the less I swallow it whole.”
