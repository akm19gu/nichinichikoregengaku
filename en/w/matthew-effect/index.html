Word of the day for March 7, 2027 (Day 157)

Matthew effect

Society

Category: Society社 会

In short

The phenomenon in which those already at an advantage gain still more advantage. The rich get richer.

The story

The sociologist Robert Merton named it in 1968, writing about science. A famous researcher tends to get more credit and citations than an unknown one for the same discovery, and the gap keeps widening. The name comes from the Gospel of Matthew: “For whoever has will be given more, and they will have an abundance. Whoever does not have, even what they have will be taken from them.”

The lens

It applies to children born early in the selection year being picked for sports teams, getting more practice, and pulling further ahead. A small difference at the start can grow far beyond any difference in ability.

Drop it casually

“The popular place has a line out the door and the one next to it is empty. A textbook Matthew effect.”
