Whistled language

Language

Category: Language言 葉

In short

A language that turns speech into whistled pitches and lengths so it can carry over long distances.

The story

On La Gomera in Spain’s Canary Islands, people use Silbo Gomero, which renders Spanish in whistles. It’s thought to have developed so people could talk across the island’s deep ravines, kilometers apart. Its speakers dwindled at one point, but it’s now taught in schools, and in 2009 UNESCO inscribed it as Intangible Cultural Heritage.

The lens

Whistled languages have been found around the world, including in a mountain village in Turkey. Language grows not only from the shape of the mouth and voice but from the shape of the land.

Drop it casually

“I want to talk to my friend in the building across the street. If only we had a whistled language, I’d just open the window.”
