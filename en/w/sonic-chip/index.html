Sonic chip

Food

Category: Food食

In short

An experiment showing that amplifying the crunch of potato chips makes them taste fresher and better.

The story

The Oxford University psychologist Charles Spence and colleagues picked up the sound of people biting chips with a microphone, altered it, and played it back through headphones. The louder the crunch and the more its high frequencies were boosted, the crisper and fresher people rated the chips. The study won an Ig Nobel Prize in 2008.

The lens

Taste isn’t decided by the tongue alone. Sound, color, the weight of the plate, the mood of the restaurant: all the senses build flavor. Even the crinkle of a snack bag may be part of the show.

Drop it casually

“These rice crackers taste better because of how they sound. Just like the sonic chip study.”
