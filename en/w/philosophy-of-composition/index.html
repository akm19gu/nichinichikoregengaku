The Philosophy of Composition

Literature

Category: Literature文 学

In short

Edgar Allan Poe’s essay arguing that poems and stories aren’t born from inspiration but are built logically, working backward from the effect.

The story

In this 1846 essay Poe explained how he wrote his poem “The Raven.” He first decided its length and the effect he wanted, chose the death of a beautiful woman as the most melancholy subject, and picked “Nevermore” as the refrain, building everything by calculation.

The lens

It’s so neatly reasoned that some suspect it was rationalized after the fact, or half a joke. Still, the idea of building a story backward from its ending lives on in how screenplays are written today.

Drop it casually

“I built this whole project backward from the final scene I wanted to show. Poe’s philosophy of composition.”
