Word of the day for October 4, 2027 (Day 368)

White coat hypertension

Medicine & Body

Category: Medicine & Body医 学か ら だ

In short

When blood pressure is normal at home but reads high at the clinic because of nerves around doctors and nurses.

The story

The name comes from blood pressure rising from tension and anxiety in front of medical staff in white coats. If it’s measured only in the exam room, unnecessary treatment may begin. That’s why readings taken at home, or by a monitor worn all day, are valued as part of the picture.

The lens

There’s also the reverse, masked hypertension, which reads normal at the clinic but high at home or work. Change where you measure and the result changes. Our bodies respond to our surroundings more than we think.

Drop it casually

“My blood pressure is high at the clinic. I want to believe it’s white coat hypertension, but I’ll check at home too, just in case.”
