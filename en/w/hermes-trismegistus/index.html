Hermes Trismegistus

Religion & Myth

Category: Religion & Myth宗 教神 話

In short

A legendary sage formed from the merger of an Egyptian god of wisdom with the Greek god Hermes, revered as the father of alchemy and mystical philosophy.

The story

The name means “thrice-great Hermes.” The writings attributed to him, the Hermetica, were read avidly in the Renaissance as ancient wisdom, until in 1614 the scholar Isaac Casaubon analyzed their style and showed they were actually written in the early centuries AD.

The lens

The airtight seal alchemists used to close their vessels was called the seal of Hermes after him. That’s why something airtight is still called “hermetic” in English.

Drop it casually

“This container seals completely. Hermetically, which is a leftover of Hermes Trismegistus.”
