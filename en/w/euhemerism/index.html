Euhemerism

Religion & Myth

Category: Religion & Myth宗 教神 話

In short

The theory that the gods of myth were originally real kings or heroes, worshipped as gods after they died.

The story

It is named after Euhemerus, a Greek writer of around the 4th and 3rd centuries BCE. In a tale called the Sacred History, he described inscriptions found on an imaginary island proving that even Zeus had once been a human king. Early Christian writers later used the idea to attack the old pagan gods.

The lens

People becoming gods is not rare. In Japan, the 9th-century scholar Sugawara no Michizane, who died in exile, came to be worshipped as Tenjin, the god of learning, and students still pray to him before exams. Reading myths with a real person in mind behind each god adds a new pleasure.

Drop it casually

“Japan’s god of exams was a real scholar who died in exile. That’s euhemerism with a happy ending.”
