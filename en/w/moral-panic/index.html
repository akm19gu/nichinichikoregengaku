Word of the day for August 3, 2027 (Day 306)

Moral panic

Society

Category: Society社 会

In short

When a group or trend is played up far beyond reality as a threat to social order and values.

The story

The British sociologist Stanley Cohen named it in a 1972 book. He analyzed how scuffles between two youth subcultures in 1960s Britain, the Mods and the Rockers, were wildly exaggerated by newspapers and stirred up anxiety across society. He called the people targeted by such uproars “folk devils.”

The lens

Novels, comics, video games, smartphones: the history of alarms that a new entertainment will corrupt the young has repeated itself again and again. Imagining how today’s uproar will look in a few decades can be calming.

Drop it casually

“Everyone says this new app will ruin kids. Could be the same moral panic as the old video game scare.”
