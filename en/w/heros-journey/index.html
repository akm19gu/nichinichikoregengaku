The hero’s journey

Literature

Category: Literature文 学

In short

The story pattern said to appear in myths and tales worldwide: the hero leaves ordinary life, passes through trials, and returns transformed.

The story

The American mythologist Joseph Campbell laid it out in his 1949 book The Hero with a Thousand Faces. Stages like the call to adventure, meeting a mentor, trials, the supreme ordeal, and returning with a reward recur, he argued, in myths across cultures. George Lucas famously drew on it when making Star Wars.

The lens

It has been absorbed into Hollywood screenwriting, and many films follow it. Knowing the pattern lets you predict what comes next, which is exactly what makes breaking it so surprising.

Drop it casually

“Leave the countryside for the big city, find a mentor, get knocked down, come home. My career change was a hero’s journey.”
