Word of the day for March 3, 2028 (Day 519)

Year 2038 problem

Math & Computing

Category: Math & Computing数 理情 報

In short

The way many computers count time hits its limit on January 19, 2038, potentially causing errors such as dates jumping back to 1901.

The story

Many computers based on UNIX count time as the number of seconds since January 1, 1970. If that number is stored as a 32-bit integer, it overflows just after 3:14:07 a.m. UTC on January 19, 2038, and wraps around to December 13, 1901.

The lens

The Year 2000 problem was overcome without major chaos thanks to many people’s painstaking preparation. That’s led some to say it was overblown, but nothing happened because someone fixed it. The next deadline is quietly approaching.

Drop it casually

“Keep using ancient machines forever and the Year 2038 problem might send their clocks back to 1901.”
