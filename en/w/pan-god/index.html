Pan

Religion & Myth

Category: Religion & Myth宗 教神 話

In short

The goat-legged, horned god of shepherds in Greek myth, and the root of the word “panic.”

The story

Pan guarded shepherds and flocks in the hills and was known for playing his reed pipes. Woken from his nap, he would shout in anger and throw flocks and people into fear for no reason. That kind of sudden, groundless terror sweeping through a crowd came to be called panic, “of Pan.”

The lens

What makes panic frightening is how it spreads through a crowd all at once, without anyone knowing why. In a packed hall or a buying frenzy, picturing the goat-legged god at work might help you keep calm.

Drop it casually

“The moment someone said stock was running out, the store went into chaos. Like Pan had just walked through.”
