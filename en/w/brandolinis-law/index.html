Word of the day for January 4, 2027 (Day 95)

Brandolini’s law

Philosophy

Category: Philosophy哲 学思 考

In short

The effort needed to refute nonsense is an order of magnitude greater than the effort needed to produce it.

The story

It spread from a 2013 social media post by the Italian programmer Alberto Brandolini, and it’s also known by a blunter name, the bullshit asymmetry principle. A plausible falsehood takes seconds to write. Showing it’s wrong, with evidence, takes research, time, and patience.

The lens

It explains in one line why rebuttals never seem to catch up with rumors. Which is also why simply not sharing something before checking its source does more good than any rebuttal.

Drop it casually

“I tried to debunk that sketchy health post and ended up reading three papers. Brandolini’s law in action.”
