Word of the day for July 4, 2028 (Day 642)

Epiphany

Literature

Category: Literature文 学

In short

A moment when a small everyday event suddenly reveals the essence of something.

The story

The word originally meant the Christian feast celebrating the manifestation of Christ to the world. As a young man, the writer James Joyce used it for moments when the essence of something is suddenly revealed in a casual remark or gesture, and he placed such moments at the heart of the stories in Dubliners and elsewhere.

The lens

A sunset on the way home or someone’s offhand comment can make something suddenly click. It teaches you to look for revelation not in great events but in small ones.

Drop it casually

“I was washing the dishes when the answer to the project hit me. An epiphany descended on the kitchen.”
