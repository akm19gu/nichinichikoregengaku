Antikythera mechanism

Technology

Category: Technology技 術発 明

In short

A geared astronomical calculator more than 2,000 years old, found in 1901 in a shipwreck off the Greek island of Antikythera.

The story

Sponge divers brought up bronze statues and a corroded lump from an ancient wreck. Hidden inside was an intricate device with more than 30 interlocking gears. Detailed study using X-rays and other methods has shown it could display the positions of the sun and moon, predict eclipses, and even track the years of the ancient Olympic Games.

The lens

Geared machinery this precise doesn’t appear again anywhere for more than a thousand years. Technology doesn’t always advance in a straight line; it can be lost. People 2,000 years ago knew far more than we tend to assume.

Drop it casually

“On my predecessor’s computer I found an amazing spreadsheet nobody knew about. Our office’s Antikythera mechanism.”
