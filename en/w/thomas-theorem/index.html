Word of the day for November 18, 2026 (Day 48)

Thomas theorem

Society

Category: Society社 会

In short

“If men define situations as real, they are real in their consequences.”

The story

It comes from a line in a 1928 book by the American sociologists W. I. Thomas and Dorothy Swaine Thomas. Whether or not a belief is true, people act on how they define the situation, and their actions create a new reality. It is quoted as a basic principle of sociology.

The lens

The sociologist Robert K. Merton built on it to coin the “self-fulfilling prophecy.” The classic example is a rumor that sends depositors rushing to a perfectly healthy bank, which then really does collapse.

Drop it casually

“Once people decide a restaurant is busy, a line actually forms. That’s the Thomas theorem.”
