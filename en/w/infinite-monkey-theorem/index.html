Infinite monkey theorem

Math & Computing

Category: Math & Computing数 理情 報

In short

The probability claim that a monkey hitting typewriter keys at random for an infinite amount of time would eventually type the complete works of Shakespeare.

The story

It’s usually traced to the French mathematician Émile Borel, who around 1913 used typing monkeys as an image for an absurdly tiny probability. In 2003 a British zoo actually put a computer in a monkey enclosure. The monkeys typed five pages consisting mostly of the letter S and nearly broke the keyboard by hitting it with a stone.

The lens

In 2024, Australian mathematicians announced that, given the real number of chimpanzees and their lifespans, there was essentially no chance of them typing Shakespeare before the universe runs out. Between “it will happen eventually” and “it will actually happen” lies a mind-boggling distance.
