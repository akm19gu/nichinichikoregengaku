Carcinization

Science

Category: Science科 学

In short

Crustaceans that are not crabs keep evolving into crab-shaped creatures, independently, over and over.

The story

The British zoologist Lancelot Borradaile named it in 1916, calling it one of nature’s many attempts to evolve a crab. King crabs, for example, are actually relatives of hermit crabs, yet they ended up with the same flat shell and folded-under tail as true crabs.

The lens

It is a textbook case of convergent evolution, where unrelated lineages arrive at the same shape. Online it lives on as the meme that everything eventually becomes a crab.

Drop it casually

“King crabs aren’t real crabs. They’re hermit crab relatives that went through carcinization.”
