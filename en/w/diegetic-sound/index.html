Diegetic sound

Art & Film

Category: Art & Film芸 術

In short

Sound in a film that the characters can hear too, as distinct from music played only for the audience from outside the story.

The story

From the Greek diegesis, “narrative.” A record a character puts on or a phone ringing in the room is sound within the story world. The score that swells in a tense scene, which only the audience hears, is non-diegetic.

The lens

When background music turns out to be coming from a character’s radio, the audience is jolted. Paying attention to who can hear a sound reveals the craft of a film.

Drop it casually

“I thought it was the soundtrack, but it was the song the hero was listening to in her earbuds. Clever use of diegetic sound.”
