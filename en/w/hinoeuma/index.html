Fire Horse year

hinoe-uma

Religion & Myth

Category: Religion & Myth宗 教神 話

In short

One of the 60 combinations in the East Asian zodiac cycle, coming around once every 60 years. In Japan, a superstition long held that girls born in this year would be hot-tempered.

The story

The superstition is said to have spread partly through a popular tale about Yaoya Oshichi, a young woman of the Edo period who set a fire hoping to see her lover again and who was said to have been born in a Fire Horse year. In the Fire Horse year of 1966, the number of births in Japan fell about 25% from the year before.

The lens

The next Fire Horse year after 1966 came around in 2026. It’s a rare case where statistics show plainly how far a superstition can move people’s behavior, and a warning against judging people’s character by the year they were born.

Drop it casually

“Judging someone’s personality by the zodiac year they were born in is no different from the Fire Horse superstition.”
