Word of the day for March 14, 2027 (Day 164)

Maxwell’s demon

Science

Category: Science科 学

In short

An imaginary being from a thought experiment: by sorting gas molecules one at a time and opening and closing a little door, could it create a temperature difference without using energy?

The story

The British physicist James Clerk Maxwell came up with it around 1867. Divide a box in two with a partition, and if a tiny being at the door lets only fast molecules through one way and slow ones the other, one side gets hot and the other cold. That seemed to violate the second law of thermodynamics, which says heat flows on its own only from hot to cold.

The lens

It took physicists close to a century to exorcise the demon. They found that telling molecules apart, remembering them, and erasing that memory all cost energy, revealing how deeply information and heat are linked.

Drop it casually

“If only there were a receptionist who let in only fast-moving people, meetings would end in no time. I want to hire Maxwell’s demon.”
