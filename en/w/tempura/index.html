Word of the day for April 28, 2028 (Day 575)

Tempura

Food

Category: Food食

In short

The Japanese dish of fish and vegetables coated in batter and deep-fried. Its name most likely comes from Portuguese.

The story

Portuguese visitors are thought to have brought battered, fried food to Japan in the 16th century. Theories about the name include the Latin tempora, for the Christian Ember Days when meat was avoided in favor of fish and vegetables, and a Portuguese word for seasoning. In the Edo period it became a popular food sold on skewers from street stalls.

The lens

Tempura, a face of Japanese cuisine, traces back to food brought by European traders. Tradition may be what something from outside looks like after it has slowly settled into a place.

Drop it casually

“The word tempura apparently comes from Portuguese. It wears a Japanese face, but it’s secretly cosmopolitan.”
