Twin Earth

Philosophy

Category: Philosophy哲 学思 考

In short

A thought experiment about meaning involving a planet identical to Earth except that the liquid called “water” there is a different substance.

The story

The American philosopher Hilary Putnam presented it in a 1975 paper. Twin Earth’s “water” looks and tastes just like ours but is chemically something else. Even if an Earthling and a Twin Earthling in an age before chemistry have the same thoughts in their heads when they say “water,” they are referring to different things. From this Putnam concluded that “meanings just ain’t in the head.”

The lens

The meaning of a word isn’t fixed only inside the speaker’s head; it depends on connections to the world and to society. It’s a clue to why people using the same words sometimes talk past each other.

Drop it casually

“When she says “right away” and when I say it, we mean different amounts of time. It’s like Twin Earth.”
