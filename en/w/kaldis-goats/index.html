Word of the day for January 18, 2028 (Day 474)

Kaldi’s goats

Food

Category: Food食

In short

The legend of Kaldi, an Ethiopian goatherd, and the discovery of coffee.

The story

One day Kaldi noticed his goats leaping about excitedly after eating the red berries of a certain shrub. A monk who heard the story tried the berries and found he stayed awake through his night prayers. The earliest confirmed appearance of the legend is in a text written in Europe in the 17th century.

The lens

The true origin is unknown, but coffee is generally agreed to have come from the Ethiopian highlands and spread to the world by way of Yemen. There are even coffee shops named after this goatherd.

Drop it casually

“I want my morning coffee to have me bouncing around like Kaldi’s goats. Or at least to keep me awake.”
