Mars Climate Orbiter

Technology

Category: Technology技 術発 明

In short

An American space probe lost in 1999 as it tried to enter orbit around Mars. The cause was mixed-up units.

The story

In calculations for the probe’s trajectory, software from the contractor that built it produced figures in imperial units, and the NASA team used them as if they were metric. Nobody caught the mismatch, and the probe came too close to Mars. It’s thought to have burned up in the atmosphere or skipped off into space.

The lens

A mission costing hundreds of millions of dollars was lost to a missed step you learn in grade school: check your units. The more advanced the technology, the more the obvious checks at the seams matter. When different teams join forces, agreeing on words and units is the first job.

Drop it casually

“Does “by tomorrow” mean tonight or tomorrow night? Let’s confirm. I don’t want a repeat of the Mars Climate Orbiter.”
