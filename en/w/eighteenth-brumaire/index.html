18 Brumaire

History

Category: History歴 史

In short

The day in 1799 when Napoleon seized power in a coup. Also known from the title of a work by Marx.

The story

Brumaire, the “month of fog,” was a month in the revolutionary calendar. On that day Napoleon overthrew the government and set up the Consulate, and he later rose to become emperor. Half a century later, after his nephew Louis Napoleon staged his own coup, Karl Marx wrote The Eighteenth Brumaire of Louis Bonaparte in 1852.

The lens

Marx opened it by writing that great historical events occur twice, the first time as tragedy, the second as farce. A line you can’t resist quoting when something looks like a rerun of the past.

Drop it casually

“Another department is redoing, exactly, the project that failed before. As The Eighteenth Brumaire says, the second time is farce.”
