Polywater

Science

Category: Science科 学

In short

A supposed polymerized form of water with strange properties that excited researchers worldwide in the 1960s, and turned out to be contaminated water.

The story

Soviet scientists reported that inside thin glass tubes they could produce an odd water that was more viscous than normal and hard to freeze. Labs in the West rushed to replicate it, and some even worried that if the world’s water turned into polywater, life on Earth would end. In the end it was found to contain material leached from the glass and contaminants such as sweat.

The lens

Once a craze takes off, even those checking it get swept up in the excitement. Within a few years almost nobody mentioned it. It teaches both how a new discovery can fire people up and how fast the fever cools.

Drop it casually

“Everyone was obsessed at first, and now nobody even brings it up. That fad was pure polywater.”
