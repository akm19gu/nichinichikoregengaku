Word of the day for December 2, 2027 (Day 427)

Bloomsday

Literature

Category: Literature文 学

In short

June 16, celebrated each year in Dublin and elsewhere in honor of James Joyce’s novel Ulysses.

The story

Ulysses follows a single day in Dublin, June 16, 1904, through the actions and thoughts of Leopold Bloom and others. The date is said to be the day of Joyce’s first outing with Nora Barnacle, who later became his wife. It began in 1954 when writers retraced the novel’s route, and today there are readings and costumed events in many places.

The lens

An enormous novel devoted to a single day. It makes you think your own ordinary day might hold a whole novel’s worth of events.

Drop it casually

“I wrote up today in a detailed diary and it got surprisingly long. My own Bloomsday.”
