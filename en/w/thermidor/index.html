Word of the day for March 30, 2028 (Day 546)

Thermidor

History

Category: History歴 史

In short

The 1794 coup in the French Revolution that toppled Robespierre, architect of the Terror. By extension, the backlash after a revolution goes too far.

The story

Thermidor, the “month of heat,” was a month in the calendar created by the revolutionary government. On the ninth of that month, Robespierre and his allies were arrested in the National Convention, and they were executed the next day. The Reign of Terror ended there.

The lens

Later thinkers generalized the word, arguing that every revolution sees a swing back after its excesses. Lobster Thermidor, incidentally, is said to take its name, by way of an 1894 play, from the same month.

Drop it casually

“After the sudden reforms, everyone started missing the old ways. The office Thermidor has arrived.”
