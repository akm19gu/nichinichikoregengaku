Aeolipile

Technology

Category: Technology技 術発 明

In short

A sphere spun by jets of steam, described by the engineer Hero of Alexandria around the first century. One of the earliest steam engines.

The story

Heat a cauldron of water and the steam is fed into a sphere above, rushing out through bent tubes and setting the sphere spinning. The name comes from Aeolus, the Greek god of the winds. Hero also described a device sometimes called the world’s first vending machine, which dispensed holy water when a coin was inserted.

The lens

The principle of moving things with steam was known nearly 2,000 years ago, yet it didn’t lead to an industrial revolution until the 18th century. One explanation is that labor was cheap and no one was looking for a use for it. An invention changes the world only when society needs it.

Drop it casually

“The idea was around for ages and nobody used it. Like the aeolipile, this proposal is waiting for the times to catch up.”
