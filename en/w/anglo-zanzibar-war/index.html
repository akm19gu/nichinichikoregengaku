Anglo-Zanzibar War

History

Category: History歴 史

In short

The 1896 war between Britain and Zanzibar, said to be the shortest war on record.

The story

When the sultan of Zanzibar in East Africa died, a claimant Britain didn’t favor declared himself successor and barricaded himself in the palace. Britain issued an ultimatum to leave, and when it expired, its ships shelled the palace. The fighting is said to have lasted about 40 minutes.

The lens

It’s usually told as a quirky record, but behind it lies the reality of imperial powers dictating even who would succeed to a throne. Good practice in looking at what sits behind an amusing number.

Drop it casually

“The shortest war ever sounds like a punch line, but the Anglo-Zanzibar War had real casualties.”
