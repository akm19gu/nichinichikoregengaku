Anagnorisis

Literature

Category: Literature文 学

In short

The moment a character in a story realizes a crucial truth they hadn’t known. Also translated as recognition or discovery.

The story

Aristotle named it in the Poetics as a key element of tragedy. The classic case is Oedipus the King: the king, hunting for a culprit to save his city, learns that he himself is the man who killed his father and married his mother. Aristotle thought tragedy is most powerful when recognition coincides with peripeteia, a reversal of fortune.

The lens

The solution of a detective story and the final twist of a movie are descendants of a device analyzed more than two thousand years ago. That “so that’s what was going on” moment has a proper name.

Drop it casually

“The moment I realized the glasses I was looking for were on my head, I had my own anagnorisis.”
