Word of the day for March 12, 2028 (Day 528)

Readymade

Art & Film

Category: Art & Film芸 術

In short

Presenting a mass-produced object, as is or barely altered, as a work of art.

The story

The French-born artist Marcel Duchamp started it. In 1917 he signed a store-bought urinal “R. Mutt,” titled it Fountain, and submitted it to an exhibition in New York, where it was refused. By challenging the very assumption that artists make their works by hand, it had an enormous influence on 20th-century art.

The lens

In recent years some have argued that the idea actually belonged to a different woman artist. It’s ironic that a work questioning who makes art carries a mystery about its own maker.

Drop it casually

“Give that mystery chair in the corner of the meeting room a title and it’s contemporary art. The readymade approach.”
