Piltdown Man

Science

Category: Science科 学

In short

A faked fossil “discovered” in England in 1912 and accepted as a human ancestor for more than 40 years.

The story

The amateur archaeologist Charles Dawson announced that he had found a humanlike skull and an apelike jawbone in a gravel pit at Piltdown in England. It fit perfectly with what scholars of the day expected, an ancestor with a big brain, and it was widely accepted. In 1953, chemical analysis revealed it to be a forgery combining a doctored human skull with an orangutan’s jaw.

The lens

It took 40 years to expose partly because it was what so many people wanted to see. It’s still told as a lesson in science: be most suspicious of evidence that fits your expectations too well.

Drop it casually

“This data fits the boss’s theory a little too perfectly. I hope it isn’t another Piltdown Man.”
