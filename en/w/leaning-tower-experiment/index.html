Galileo’s Leaning Tower experiment

Galileo’s Leaning Tower of Pisa experiment

Science

Category: Science科 学

In short

The famous story that Galileo dropped balls of different weights from the Leaning Tower of Pisa to show they hit the ground together. Whether he actually did it is doubtful.

The story

The tale spread through a biography by Vincenzo Viviani, Galileo’s pupil in his later years, but Galileo’s own writings contain no record of an experiment at the tower. His challenge to the idea, going back to Aristotle, that heavier things fall faster was made mainly through thought experiments and by rolling balls down ramps. The Dutch scholar Simon Stevin had already dropped balls of different weights from a height before him.

The lens

There are many “great moments” of scientific heroes that may be later embellishments. None of that changes the fact that Galileo’s ideas were right. It’s worth enjoying the story and the facts separately.

Drop it casually

“Galileo may never have done the Leaning Tower experiment. The more iconic the scene, the more suspicious.”
