Columbian exchange

History

Category: History歴 史

In short

The vast transfer of crops, livestock, diseases, and people between the Americas and Europe, Asia, and Africa after 1492.

The story

The American historian Alfred Crosby named it in a 1972 book. Potatoes, corn, tomatoes, and chili peppers went to the Old World; wheat, horses, cattle, and sugarcane to the New. Diseases such as smallpox also came to the Americas, and Indigenous populations collapsed.

The lens

Before this exchange there were no tomatoes in Italian cooking and no chilies in Indian curry. Many “traditional” flavors turn out to be surprisingly new.

Drop it casually

“This spaghetti’s tomatoes and chili flakes both come from the Americas. A child of the Columbian exchange.”
