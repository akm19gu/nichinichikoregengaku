Lilith

Religion & Myth

Category: Religion & Myth宗 教神 話

In short

In Jewish tradition, Adam’s first wife, created before Eve.

The story

Genesis contains two accounts: one in which God creates male and female together, and one in which Eve is made from Adam’s rib. The medieval text known as the Alphabet of Ben Sira explained the discrepancy with the story of a first wife, Lilith, made from the same earth as Adam. Lilith insisted on being Adam’s equal and left Eden, and she later came to be feared as a demon of the night.

The lens

Long portrayed as a demon, Lilith was reread in the 20th century as a symbol of the independent woman who won’t submit to men. The same story can make someone a hero or a villain depending on the age.

Drop it casually

“Refuse to do as you’re told and you get cast as the villain. Nothing’s changed since Lilith.”
