Lobbyist

Politics & Law

Category: Politics & Law政 治法

In short

Someone who works on lawmakers and officials to steer policy and legislation in their favor.

The story

The word comes from petitioners lying in wait for legislators in the lobby outside the chamber. A famous story says it began with President Ulysses S. Grant calling the favor-seekers who gathered in the lobby of Washington’s Willard Hotel “lobbyists,” but the word was in use earlier, including around the British Parliament.

The lens

It’s also an example of a famous origin story that turns out to be a well-crafted myth. When you hear where a word comes from, you start wanting to check whether that origin is true too.

Drop it casually

“Someone’s working on each of us one by one to pick the restaurant for drinks. A true lobbyist.”
