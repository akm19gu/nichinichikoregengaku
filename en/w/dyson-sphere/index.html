Dyson sphere

Space

Category: Space宇 宙

In short

A hypothetical megastructure that an advanced civilization might build around a star to capture all of its energy.

The story

The American physicist Freeman Dyson proposed it in a 1960 paper. If such a structure existed, it would block the star’s light and give off the energy as heat in infrared. So searching for strange objects glowing strongly in infrared, he suggested, might reveal civilizations in space.

The lens

In 2015 astronomers found Tabby’s Star, whose brightness dips irregularly and dramatically, and people wondered whether it might be a Dyson sphere. Later observations point to dust as the likely cause. The hunt for aliens is also a hunt for unexpected natural phenomena.

Drop it casually

“Solar chargers lined up all over my room so not a drop of sunlight escapes. My own Dyson sphere.”
