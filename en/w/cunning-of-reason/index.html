Cunning of reason

List der Vernunft

Philosophy

Category: Philosophy哲 学思 考

In short

Hegel’s idea that even as individuals act on their desires and passions in history, the ends of “reason” are realized through them.

The story

Hegel held that history is driven by the ambitions and passions of great figures, but that behind them reason makes use of those passions to realize its own purposes. He famously wrote in a letter after glimpsing Napoleon that he had seen the world spirit on horseback; great individuals were, in his view, also tools used by reason.

The lens

Something you start for your own reasons can become part of a much larger current. Many find the idea far too mystical, but it adds depth to how you look at history.

Drop it casually

“I started that project just to get noticed, and it ended up changing how the whole company works. The cunning of reason, maybe.”
