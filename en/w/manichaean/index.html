Manichaean

Religion & Myth

Category: Religion & Myth宗 教神 話

In short

A view of the world as an all-out battle between good and evil, light and darkness.

The story

Manichaeism, founded by Mani in third-century Persia, taught that two principles, light and darkness, are at war within the world, and at one time it spread from the Roman Empire in the west to China in the east. The Christian theologian Augustine was a Manichaean in his youth.

The lens

Portraying opponents as pure evil and yourself as pure good in politics or debate is still criticized in English as Manichaean. Just when you most want everything in black and white, it’s worth looking for the gray.

Drop it casually

“Both sides in this argument are treating the other as absolute evil. Way too Manichaean.”
