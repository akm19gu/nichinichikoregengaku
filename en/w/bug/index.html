Word of the day for March 5, 2028 (Day 521)

Bug

Technology

Category: Technology技 術発 明

In short

A defect in a computer program or machine. Literally, an insect.

The story

In 1947, a team at Harvard running a computer, including Grace Hopper, found a moth trapped inside the machine. They taped it into the logbook with the note “First actual case of bug being found.” The logbook is still preserved in an American museum.

The lens

The story is often told as the origin of the word, but the note itself shows “bug” was already in use. Indeed, Thomas Edison called a machine’s glitches “bugs” in letters in the 1870s. The moth wasn’t the origin, just a clever pun.

Drop it casually

“I almost said the word bug comes from a moth, then stopped myself. Machines have had bugs in them since Edison’s day.”
