Voyager Golden Record

Space

Category: Space宇 宙

In short

A record of sounds and images from Earth carried aboard the Voyager 1 and 2 probes, launched in 1977. A letter to anyone out there we might someday meet.

The story

The astronomer Carl Sagan led the team that chose its contents. It holds greetings in 55 languages, sounds such as waves and birdsong, 115 images, and music from around the world. From Japan came the shakuhachi piece “Tsuru no Sugomori” (Cranes in Their Nest). Its surface is engraved with symbols and diagrams explaining how to play it.

The lens

It also contains the brain waves of Ann Druyan, who worked on the project. Just before the recording, she and Sagan had agreed to marry. A record of a human brain in love is still traveling beyond the solar system.

Drop it casually

“If aliens ever pick up the Golden Record, I wonder how the shakuhachi will sound to them.”
