Word of the day for March 18, 2028 (Day 534)

Ether Day

Medicine & Body

Category: Medicine & Body医 学か ら だ

In short

October 16, 1846, when an operation under ether anesthesia was successfully performed in public in Boston.

The story

At Massachusetts General Hospital, the dentist William Morton put a patient to sleep with ether, and a surgeon removed a tumor from his neck. Seeing the patient feel no pain, the surgeon reportedly declared, “Gentlemen, this is no humbug.” This public operation launched the spread of anesthesia around the world.

The lens

Yet in Japan, more than 40 years earlier in 1804, the physician Hanaoka Seishū of Kii Province had used an herbal anesthetic of his own invention to put a patient under general anesthesia and successfully operate on breast cancer. Because Japan had little contact with the outside world, the achievement long went unknown abroad.

Drop it casually

“Grateful for the anesthesia at the dentist. And proud that a general-anesthesia operation succeeded in Japan before Ether Day.”
