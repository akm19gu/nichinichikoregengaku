Round character

Literature

Category: Literature文 学

In short

A character too complex to sum up in a single trait, with unexpected sides that surprise the reader. The opposite is a flat character.

The story

The English novelist E. M. Forster drew the distinction in his 1927 lecture collection Aspects of the Novel. A flat character can be summed up in one sentence; a round one, he said, is “capable of surprising in a convincing way.”

The lens

Forster didn’t dismiss flat characters; he thought they had their uses, especially in supporting roles. Try counting how many times a character surprises you over the course of a story.

Drop it casually

“This villain’s past got revealed at the end and he suddenly became a round character.”
