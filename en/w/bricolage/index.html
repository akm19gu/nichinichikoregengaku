Word of the day for November 11, 2027 (Day 406)

Bricolage

Society

Category: Society社 会

In short

Making what you need by combining whatever materials and tools happen to be on hand.

The story

The French anthropologist Claude Lévi-Strauss used the term in his 1962 book The Savage Mind. He contrasted the “engineer,” who procures parts to fit a blueprint, with the bricoleur, the handyman who cleverly combines what’s already at hand, and saw in the latter the character of mythical thought.

The lens

Making dinner from leftovers in the fridge or rigging up a tool from spare parts is proper bricolage. You can also read it as the wisdom of starting without waiting for perfect materials.

Drop it casually

“Three dishes made entirely from fridge leftovers. Tonight’s dinner is a masterpiece of bricolage.”
