Martian canals

Space

Category: Space宇 宙

In short

A network of lines that astronomers in the late 19th century believed they saw on the surface of Mars, sparking talk of canals built by Martians.

The story

In 1877 the Italian astronomer Giovanni Schiaparelli called the lines he saw on Mars canali, Italian for “channels.” In English the word was taken as “canals,” implying something artificial, and imaginations ran wild about a Martian civilization. The American businessman Percival Lowell spent his own fortune building an observatory and devoted his life to observing the canals.

The lens

In 1965 the Mariner 4 probe sent back photos of Mars showing no canals at all, only a cratered wasteland. The canals were an illusion produced by the limits of telescopes and the human eye and mind. How one word was understood fed half a century of dreams.

Drop it casually

“One vague word gets read the wrong way and the story keeps growing. Like the Martian canals.”
