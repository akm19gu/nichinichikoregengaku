Word of the day for May 22, 2027 (Day 233)

Kalpa

Religion & Myth

Category: Religion & Myth宗 教神 話

In short

An unimaginably long unit of time used in Buddhism and other religions of India.

The story

From the Sanskrit kalpa. Buddhist scriptures compare it to a vast rock that a celestial being brushes with a thin robe once every hundred years: even when the rock is worn away, the kalpa still isn’t over. In Hinduism, one day of Brahma, the creator god, is a kalpa, said to be about 4.32 billion years.

The lens

It lives on in everyday Japanese. The word okkū, “can’t be bothered,” is written “a hundred million kalpas,” the feeling of facing something that seems to take forever.

Drop it casually

“Clearing this pile of paperwork will take a kalpa. Just thinking about it makes me tired.”
