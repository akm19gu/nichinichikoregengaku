Word of the day for September 5, 2027 (Day 339)

Invented tradition

Society

Category: Society社 会

In short

When a tradition that seems ancient was actually created fairly recently.

The story

The historians Eric Hobsbawm and Terence Ranger explored it in a 1983 collection of the same name. One example: the short kilt seen as a symbol of Scotland is said by one account to have been devised in the 18th century by an English factory owner as work clothing, and the system of tartans for each clan was codified in the 19th century.

The lens

A tradition being new doesn’t make it worthless, but “it’s always been this way” is worth questioning once. Many of the things called “traditional” in Japan, too, took shape only after the 1868 Meiji Restoration.

Drop it casually

“They call this event a company tradition, but apparently it started five years ago. An invented tradition.”
