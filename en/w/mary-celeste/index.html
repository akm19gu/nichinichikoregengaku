Mary Celeste

History

Category: History歴 史

In short

A sailing ship found drifting in the Atlantic in 1872 with not a single person aboard.

The story

When it was found, the ship was seaworthy and its cargo of alcohol was nearly intact, but the lifeboat was gone, and the captain, his family, and the crew had vanished. What happened to them is still unknown. A young Arthur Conan Doyle wrote a short story based on the case, and details he invented spread as if they were fact.

The lens

Many famous details, such as half-eaten meals left on the table, are said to be fictions popularized by Doyle’s story. The more mysterious the case, the more you should watch out for stories added later.

Drop it casually

“I came in this morning and there were computers and coffee everywhere but no people. Creepy, like the Mary Celeste.”
