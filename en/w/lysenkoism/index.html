Word of the day for November 29, 2026 (Day 59)

Lysenkoism

History

Category: History歴 史

In short

The distortion of science when politically desired conclusions are put ahead of evidence.

The story

The Soviet agronomist Trofim Lysenko rejected Mendelian genetics and claimed that traits acquired from the environment could be inherited. Backed by Stalin’s regime, his ideas became official science. Geneticists who opposed him lost their jobs, and some were imprisoned and died. His agricultural policies are blamed for serious damage to farming in the Soviet Union and China.

The lens

Scientists still cite it as the gravest warning against research whose conclusion is decided in advance. In one word it explains why science must keep its distance from politics.

Drop it casually

“Picking the data that matches what the bosses want to hear? That’s a little Lysenkoism.”
