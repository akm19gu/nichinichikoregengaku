War of Jenkins’ Ear

History

Category: History歴 史

In short

A war between Britain and Spain that began in 1739. One pretext for it was a sea captain’s severed ear.

The story

In 1731, Robert Jenkins, captain of a British merchant ship, reportedly had his ear cut off when Spanish coast guards searched his vessel in the Caribbean. Years later, as trade tensions with Spain deepened, he was called before Parliament and is said to have displayed the preserved ear. Public fury followed, and war broke out in 1739. The memorable name was given later by the historian Thomas Carlyle.

The lens

The real cause was a fight over trading rights, but what moved people was one vivid symbol. The pattern of a big conflict catching fire on a small, easy-to-grasp story still shows up in today’s news.

Drop it casually

“A tiny mix-up set off the feud that had been smoldering between departments. Like the War of Jenkins’ Ear.”
