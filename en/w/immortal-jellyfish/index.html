Immortal jellyfish

Turritopsis

Living Things

Category: Living Things生 き 物

In short

A tiny jellyfish that, after reaching maturity, can turn back into its juvenile polyp stage when it ages or is injured, and start life over.

The story

It’s only a few millimeters to about a centimeter across. Most jellyfish die after spawning, but Turritopsis can curl into a ball, sink to the seafloor, and restart as a young polyp. Italian researchers reported this rejuvenation in the 1990s, and it became known as the immortal jellyfish. In Japan, Shin Kubota of Kyoto University succeeded in making a single one rejuvenate again and again.

The lens

Of course it still dies if it’s eaten or gets sick, so it doesn’t really live forever. Even so, this small creature shakes our assumption that aging only runs one way.

Drop it casually

“On Monday mornings I want to pull an immortal jellyfish, turn young again, and start over.”
