Ketchup

Food

Category: Food食

In short

The name of the tomato condiment, which originally meant an Asian fish sauce with no tomatoes in it at all.

The story

The word is thought to come from a word in the Hokkien language of southern China for a sauce made from salted fish. Around the 17th century, British traders in Southeast Asia brought it home, and in Britain sauces made from mushrooms, walnuts, oysters and more were called ketchup. Recipes for tomato ketchup appeared in early 19th-century America.

The lens

Today ketchup means tomato and nothing else. A food’s name can outlive a complete change of contents. Inside that bottle is the history of a word that crossed oceans.

Drop it casually

“The ancestor of the ketchup on my fries was a fish sauce. Makes it look like a cousin of fish sauce in Thai food.”
