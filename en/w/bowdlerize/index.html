Bowdlerize

Literature

Category: Literature文 学

In short

To gut a work by cutting or rewriting anything considered immoral or vulgar.

The story

It’s named after Thomas Bowdler, an English physician who in 1818 published The Family Shakespeare, a complete works with sexual references and blasphemy removed so families could read it safely. It sold well, but it later became a byword for distorting a work by tampering with it.

The lens

Much of the work on the first edition is said to have been done by his sister Henrietta. When you read a gentle children’s version of a classic or watch a film edited for TV, you may start wondering what was cut.

Drop it casually

“The TV version of this movie cuts out a crucial scene entirely. Thoroughly bowdlerized.”
