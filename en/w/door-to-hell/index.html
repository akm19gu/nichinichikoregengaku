Door to Hell

Darvaza gas crater

Geography & Earth

Category: Geography & Earth地 理地 球

In short

A huge crater in the desert of Turkmenistan in Central Asia that is said to have been burning for more than half a century.

The story

As the story usually goes, in 1971 Soviet geologists drilling for natural gas caused the ground to collapse, opening a large pit. To keep toxic gas from spreading, they set it alight, expecting it to burn out in a few weeks, but the flames are said to have kept burning ever since. The sight of fire flickering in a pit about 70 meters across earned it the name Door to Hell, and it became a tourist attraction.

The lens

In the town of Centralia, Pennsylvania, a mine fire that started underground in 1962 is still smoldering. A small fire lit by people can grow beyond their control.

“An argument we started casually has been burning for three hours. The meeting room has become the Door to Hell.”
