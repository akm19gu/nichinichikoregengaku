Roman concrete

Technology

Category: Technology技 術発 明

In short

The concrete of ancient Rome, so durable that some of its buildings still stand nearly 2,000 years later.

The story

The unreinforced concrete dome of the Pantheon in Rome, built in the second century, still stands and remains among the largest of its kind in the world. Roman concrete was made by mixing volcanic ash with lime and other ingredients, and harbor structures exposed to seawater are known to have actually grown stronger over time.

The lens

In 2023 researchers reported that small lumps of lime left in the concrete reacted with water seeping into cracks and sealed them on their own. Technology from 2,000 years ago outlasts modern concrete that deteriorates in decades. Old techniques can hold answers for the future.

Drop it casually

“Grandma’s old pot has outlasted every new appliance we’ve bought. Our household’s Roman concrete.”
