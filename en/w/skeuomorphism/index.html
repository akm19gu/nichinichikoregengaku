Skeuomorphism

Technology

Category: Technology技 術発 明

In short

Keeping the shape or look of an old tool in a new tool or design, even though it no longer serves its original purpose.

The story

The “save” icon on computers is shaped like a floppy disk, which you hardly ever see anymore. Smartphone cameras make a shutter click though they have no shutter. Page-turning animations in e-readers and old apps with wood-grain and leather textures are examples too.

The lens

Borrowing familiar old forms is a shortcut to getting people comfortable with new technology. But for generations who never knew the original object, it becomes a symbol whose shape makes no sense. There are kids who’ve never seen a floppy disk yet know it means “save.”

Drop it casually

“A junior colleague asked what the save button is supposed to be. I started with an explanation of skeuomorphism.”
