The croissant legend

Food

Category: Food食

In short

The famous tale that the croissant was invented to celebrate Vienna’s defeat of the Ottoman siege in 1683, shaped like the crescent on the enemy’s flags.

The story

The story is well known, but no records from the time support it. Crescent-shaped bread, the Kipferl, was already being eaten in Austria before then. The croissant as we know it, made from dough laminated with lots of butter, is thought to have developed in France in the 19th century, helped along by a Viennese-style bakery opening in Paris.

The lens

Heroic stories often get attached to foods after the fact. The tale of a victory pastry may just be one more seasoning to make eating it more fun.

Drop it casually

“A pastry baked to celebrate a victory? That’s just the croissant legend. What matters is whether it’s fresh out of the oven.”
