Haussmann’s renovation of Paris

Architecture & Cities

Category: Architecture & Cities建 築都 市

In short

The massive rebuilding of Paris in the mid-19th century, led by Baron Haussmann, prefect of the Seine, that remade the city from the ground up.

The story

Under Emperor Napoleon III, Georges-Eugène Haussmann spent about 17 years from 1853 tearing down narrow, tangled medieval streets and laying out straight boulevards, parks, and water and sewer systems. The rows of stone buildings of matching height that look so Parisian today took shape then.

The lens

The wide boulevards are also said to have been meant to make barricades harder to build and to let troops move quickly. It reminds you that a beautiful cityscape and urban planning as a tool of rule can be two sides of the same coin.

Drop it casually

“The redevelopment wiped out the alleys and put in a straight avenue. A little Haussmannization of our own.”
