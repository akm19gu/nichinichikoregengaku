The dog that didn’t bark

Literature

Category: Literature文 学

In short

The idea that something failing to happen, when it should have, can be a crucial clue in itself.

The story

In Arthur Conan Doyle’s 1892 story “Silver Blaze,” Sherlock Holmes points to “the curious incident of the dog in the night-time.” When the inspector replies that the dog did nothing in the night-time, Holmes says, “That was the curious incident.” The dog didn’t bark because the intruder was someone it knew well.

The lens

Noticing what didn’t happen is much harder than noticing what did. A message that should have arrived and didn’t, an objection that should have been raised and wasn’t: sometimes silence speaks loudest.

Drop it casually

“The boss who always complains said nothing about this proposal. The dog that didn’t bark. Something’s up.”
