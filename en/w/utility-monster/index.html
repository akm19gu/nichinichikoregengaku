Word of the day for September 10, 2027 (Day 344)

Utility monster

Philosophy

Category: Philosophy哲 学思 考

In short

A thought experiment: if a “monster” could feel vastly more happiness than anyone else, should everything be given to it to maximize total happiness?

The story

The American philosopher Robert Nozick presented it in his 1974 book Anarchy, State, and Utopia. Push utilitarianism, which counts increasing society’s total happiness as the good, to its limit, he argued, and any sacrifice by everyone else can be justified.

The lens

Increasing the sum of everyone’s happiness doesn’t necessarily mean treating each person well. It makes you ask whether debates over budgets and priorities are looking only at totals.

Drop it casually

“We’re putting the whole budget into that project because it makes one person so happy? Feels like feeding a utility monster.”
